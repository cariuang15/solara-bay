Solara Bay is a 2D top-down open-world crime game: explore a coastal city on foot or by car, steal cars, outrun the police across 5 wanted levels, and complete 6 story missions for Mama Rosa. Built for the Make GTA 6 bounty — 100 places paid, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard and mouse: WASD to move and drive, E to enter a car, F to punch, click to shoot, Shift to run, P to pause, H for help, M for sound.
